OUR BEGINNINGS

University History

The University of Abra traces its roots back to 1908, when it began as an industrial school established by American educator Amos Allen. Its establishment marked the beginning of an institution dedicated to education and community development.

Throughout the years, the institution experienced several transformations as it expanded its educational programs and services to meet the needs of the people of Abra.

1908 – The Beginning

The institution started as an industrial school founded by Amos Allen.

1911 – Lagangilang Farm School

The school was given the name Lagangilang Farm School.

Development Through the Years

The institution evolved into Lagangilang Agricultural School, Lagangilang National Agricultural School, Lagangilang Agricultural College, and eventually the Abra State Institute of Sciences and Technology (ASIST).

2021 – Republic Act No. 11574

Republic Act No. 11574 was approved on July 23, 2021, providing for the conversion of ASIST into the University of Abra.

2026 – University of Abra

The institution officially celebrated its transition and launch as the University of Abra in October 2026.

OUR ASPIRATION

University Vision

A university that produces quality graduates who are academically competitive, globally responsive and locally sustained.

OUR COMMITMENT

University Mission

We are committed to be agents in the development of Abra through enhanced instruction, creative and innovative researches and projects for public and community services towards globally competitive professionals who contribute to the realization of a nation that enjoys strongly rooted comfortable and secure life.

OUR DIRECTION

Goals and Objectives

The University of Abra aims to strengthen its role in education, research, extension, production, and institutional development.

  1. To produce quality graduates who are globally competitive.
  2. To develop and generate new knowledge and verify client-oriented technologies and other solutions to development problems.
  3. To disseminate and showcase client-responsive technologies and other solutions to development problems towards improved welfare of local communities.
  4. To engage in viable income-generating projects to augment the finances of the University.
  5. To transform the University of Abra into a dynamic and responsive learning and performing organization that efficiently and effectively manages its resources.
